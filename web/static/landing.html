Food–Drug ML model with SHAP

A machine-learning system that predicts whether a drug and a food compound should be avoided together, taken together, or are non-significant — with calibrated probabilities, dataset evidence, and SHAP-based explanations.

About model Predict
1,359 clean drugs
4,877 food compounds
18 selected pair features
5 trained classifiers

Predictions are generated by a FastAPI backend running the trained LightGBM model, a curated clinical rule layer, and exact-pair dataset evidence.

Research tool; confirm with a physician or pharmacist.
